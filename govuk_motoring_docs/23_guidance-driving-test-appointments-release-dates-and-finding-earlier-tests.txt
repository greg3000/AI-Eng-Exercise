Appointments are currently available until 25 April 2027

The Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) releases a new week of driving test appointments at 6am every Monday (including on bank holidays).

These appointments are 24 weeks in the future.

Earlier driving test appointments are not released at 6am on Mondays. They can become available at any time during the week.

Finding an earlier driving test appointment

Earlier appointments are not released when new appointments are added at 6am on Monday.

Earlier appointments can become available at any time when:

- someone cancels their driving test

- someone changes their driving test appointment

- 
DVSA adds additional examiner availability

There is no best time to look for an earlier appointment.

If you already have a driving test booked and want an earlier appointment, keep checking the service to change your driving test appointment.

6am on Mondays

You may be placed in a queue when the service is busy.

The queue is usually longest when appointments 24 weeks away are released at 6am on Mondays.

If you are looking for an appointment sooner than 24 weeks away, there is no benefit in joining the queue at 6am on Monday.

Joining the queue at 6am on Mondays if you do not want a test 24 weeks away increases the queuing time for people who want a test then.

How often you can search

You should not repeatedly search the service over a short period.

Do not use more than one device to try and access the service at the same time.

If you search too often, you may:

- receive a ‘search limit reached’ message

- be temporarily unable to search again

- receive an Error 15 message

Find out what to do if you see these error messages.

What you need to know about booking driving tests

Once you select a driving test appointment and accept the timing warning, you have 15 minutes to complete your booking.

The appointment might no longer be available if you do not complete your booking within this time.

The service will sign you out if you are inactive for 30 minutes.

There is no waiting list at most driving test centres

There is no waiting list or cancellation list for driving tests at most test centres.

At some test centres which do not open every day, you might have the option to put your test ‘on hold’. This means that DVSA will try and find a suitable date for you. They will tell you by email if they find one.

You will be be shown this option if it’s available at the test centre you want to take your test at.

DVSA cannot send notifications

DVSA cannot notify you when new or earlier appointments become available.

Contacting DVSA will not help you get an earlier appointment

DVSA customer service agents cannot create new appointments. They can only see the same appointments you can book in the booking service.

Service availability

The online service is available from 6am to 11:40pm, 7 days a week.

Planned maintenance

At times, the service may be unavailable because planned maintenance work is taking place.

There is currently no planned maintenance.
